Tech enabling tech — one chain, working backward

Standing on old fire

Every technology sits on top of an older one. Uber couldn’t exist without the smartphone. The smartphone couldn’t exist without GPS. Click back through the chain, one layer at a time, to where it actually starts.

Layer 1 of 7 revealed

Seven layers down. None of these technologies planned for what they'd eventually enable. Fire wasn't invented to eventually give rise to ride-hailing apps. But each layer made the next one cheaper and easier to build, and that compounding is most of the story: get one general-purpose technology working, and it quietly becomes the floor everything after it stands on.

Chain drawn from Mustafa Suleyman, The Coming Wave, ch. 2 (Uber → smartphone → GPS → satellites → rockets → combustion → language and fire), with dates added.
